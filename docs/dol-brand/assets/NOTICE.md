# ⚠️ DOL Seal — Usage Restrictions (read before using)

`dol-seal.svg` is the official Seal of the U.S. Department of Labor.

**Copyright status:** Public domain as a U.S. Government work (17 U.S.C. § 105).
**But public domain ≠ free to use.** Federal agency seals are separately and
criminally restricted:

- **18 U.S.C. § 506** — forging/altering a department or agency seal: fine
  and/or up to 5 years imprisonment (doubled when used to facilitate a
  fraudulent federal-benefit application).
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/506
- **18 U.S.C. § 1017** — wrongful/fraudulent use of a government seal: fine
  and/or up to 5 years imprisonment.
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1017

**What this means for this app:**
- Do **not** wire this seal into a deployed, non-official build. Using it in a
  way that implies DOL endorsement, sponsorship, or that the app is an official
  DOL product is exactly what the statutes prohibit.
- It lives in `docs/` (a **non-served** reference folder), not `public/`, on
  purpose — so it is not served by the app by default.
- For a real DOL engagement, official marks, the approved logo lockup, and any
  seal-use authorization come **through DOL/OASAM**, not from Wikimedia.
